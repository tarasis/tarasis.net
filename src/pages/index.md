# Welcome to The Rebuild of TDN, V5

A rebuild of [tarasis.net](https://tarasis.net) a domain I've had since 2002. It has
nearly always been a blog or landing page; using Wordpress, Jeykll, Eleventy, and now
Astro (V5 beta).

I considered a straight html/css page, but with duplicated elements (like blog pages),
a desire to make it a more personal site inspired by two talks by [Sophie
Koonin](https://localghost.dev), I decided to have a play with Astro rather than
Eleventy and play with modern CSS features. The talks I mentioned:

* ["Sophie Koonin - Building websites like it’s 1999… in 2022" - State of the
Browser](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Ks1aIchFiI)
* ["Sophie Koonin – This Website Is under Construction, a Love Letter to the
	Personal Website" – btconf](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2ZUqa-lTbnU)

Who am I? Robert McGovern. 48yo confused man who is struggling to work, dealing
with depression, and currently struggling with alcoholism. So as part of my
process of recovery I am going to be working on a rebuild of this site, and "do it
in public". Although not streamed :)

I've made some conscious choices that I want to:

* Multi theme switcher
* Use YAML rather than JSON for some data files
* While I can use remapping of frontmatter on older blog posts, I am going to redo
some of frontmatter
* The blog will not just be about tech stuff, it will include notes, and stuff about
health, depression alcoholism and other struggles
* Explore pulling in data from Spotify and other APIs, so the page reflects more
me
* I want to have a /now page.
* Link to / list work I've done
* Bring in fav photos, or most recent ones
* Bring in old posts from Livejournal and WaybackMachine. (my old WP DB is sadly
lost)

So yes, its going to be a bit busy but hopefully not cluttered.
